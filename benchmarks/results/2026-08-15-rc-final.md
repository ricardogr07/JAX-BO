# Phase 5b benchmark evidence: 2026-08-15 RC

Four clean benchmark sessions ran from the existing `.venv` with:

```text
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pytest benchmarks -q --benchmark-json=benchmarks/results/2026-08-15-rc-runN.json
```

All four runs collected and passed 11 benchmarks. Raw artifacts:
`2026-08-15-rc-run1.json` through `2026-08-15-rc-run4.json`.

The RC summary median is the median of the four per-run medians. IQR values
are per-run IQRs in run order. The cross-session noise band is
`(max median - min median) / RC median`; no runs were filtered, including the
high-variance first session. Ratios are RC median divided by the comparison
baseline. A ratio below 1.00 is faster.

| Bench | RC median | IQR by run | Noise band | vs 2026-07-28 | vs v0.2.0 |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| train warm, n=32 | 16.53 ms | 2.03 / 4.65 / 1.11 / 1.93 ms | 53.7% | 0.19x | 0.31x |
| train warm, n=128 | 110.84 ms | 11.60 / 7.42 / 2.16 / 6.16 ms | 55.4% | 0.62x | 0.80x |
| train warm, n=512 | 715.70 ms | 45.04 / 32.37 / 39.03 / 254.69 ms | 31.4% | 0.29x | 0.40x |
| train fresh, n=128 | 101.37 ms | 66.82 / 13.29 / 12.75 / 71.44 ms | 95.0% | 0.16x | 0.20x |

The 2026-07-28 and v0.2.0 baselines are documented in
`benchmarks/results/2026-07-28-baseline.md` and
`benchmarks/results/2026-08-01-v0.2.0-baseline.md`. The older baseline has no
`score_candidates` row, so that comparison is N/A. Cross-session bands are
wide, especially for fresh training and the consumer path; differences inside
the band are not treated as meaningful regressions.
